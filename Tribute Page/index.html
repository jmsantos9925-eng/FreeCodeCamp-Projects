Eugene Roshal

The programmer who changed how millions of people compress and manage files.

Who is Eugene Roshal?

Eugene Roshal is a software engineer best known for creating the RAR archive file format and developing WinRAR. His work made it easier for people to compress large files, organize data, and transfer files between computers.

Timeline

1972

Eugene Roshal was born in Russia.

1993

The RAR archive file format was introduced.

1995

WinRAR was first released for Windows.

Later Years

WinRAR continued to improve and became one of the most recognizable file compression programs in the world.

Today

The RAR format and WinRAR continue to be used by millions of computer users around the world.

Why His Work Matters

Eugene Roshal created something simple but incredibly useful. File compression helps people save storage space, organize files, and send large amounts of data more efficiently.

WinRAR also became an iconic piece of computer software and has remained familiar to computer users for decades.

Quick Facts

  • Creator of the RAR archive format.
  • Original developer of WinRAR.
  • RAR stands for Roshal Archive.
  • WinRAR was first released during the 1990s.
  • RAR files are still commonly used today.

Eugene Roshal's work shows how one piece of software can remain useful for generations of computer users.
